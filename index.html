How to Play

You're given a START country and a DESTINATION country. Enter countries one at a time to build a path between them — you can only move to a country that's geographically connected to your current one.

Your goal is to reach the destination using the fewest possible countries. Unnecessary detours cost you score.

Land connections

Two countries are connected whenever they share a land border — Canada → United States, United States → Mexico, and so on.

Sea connections

Countries that don't share a land border can still be connected across a short, well-known sea crossing or strait — the UK and France across the Channel, Spain and Morocco across Gibraltar, Russia and the United States across the Bering Strait. A handful of longer "gateway" links (Iceland–UK, Australia–New Zealand) are included too, since those nations would otherwise have no route to the rest of the world at all. This list is fixed and curated, not "anything nearby" — see the project README for the full rule and the complete list.

The optimal route is calculated before you start, but never shown — only the number of moves it takes. You can Give Up at any point to reveal it.

Hints cost points: a hint tells you how many countries remain on the optimal route from where you are, without naming them.
